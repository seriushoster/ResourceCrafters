3D Totem Creator

Turn your Minecraft skin into a 3D Totem of Undying

Java Edition only. This does not work on Bedrock, consoles, or phones.

1Choose your skin

Your picture never leaves your device. Everything happens in your browser.

2Download

3. Put it in Minecraft
  1. Open Minecraft Java Edition, then go to Options > Resource Packs.
  2. Click Open Pack Folder.
  3. Drag the downloaded zip into that folder. Do not unzip it.
  4. Back in Minecraft, hover over the pack and click the arrow to move it to the right side. Click Done.
  5. Hold a Totem of Undying. It should look like you!
Not working? Check these
  • The pack is not in the list: the zip must sit directly in the pack folder, and you must be on Java Edition.
  • It says the pack is for another version: it was made for Minecraft 1.21.4 and usually still works if you turn it on anyway.
  • The file will not open here: make sure it is a .png that is 64x64 pixels.
  • Skins with thin "slim" arms may look slightly off.
